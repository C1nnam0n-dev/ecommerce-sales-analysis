# E-Commerce Sales Analysis
# Project Overview

This project explores an e-commerce sales dataset containing information about products, sales, revenue, regions, payment methods, customer ratings, and delivery times.

The goal of the project was to use Python and data analysis techniques to identify patterns in sales performance and generate useful business insights from the data.

The analysis was completed using **Python in Jupyter Notebook through Visual Studio Code**.

# Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Understand overall sales and revenue performance
- Identify the strongest-performing product categories
- Compare revenue across different regions
- Analyse revenue trends over time
- Determine which payment methods generate the most revenue
- Compare customer ratings across product categories
- Analyse delivery times across categories and regions
- Identify relationships between regions and product categories

## Analysis Performed
# 1. Revenue by Product Category

The revenue generated by each product category was analysed to determine which categories contribute the most to overall sales.

# 2. Revenue by Region

Revenue was compared across the different regions to identify the strongest-performing geographical market.

# 3. Revenue Over Time

Monthly revenue was analysed to identify changes and fluctuations in sales performance over time.

# 4. Revenue by Payment Method

The analysis compared revenue generated through:

- Card
- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Wallet

Card payments generated the highest revenue.

# 5. Customer Ratings by Product Category

Average customer ratings were compared across product categories to identify differences in customer satisfaction.

The ratings were relatively close across all categories, with Clothing having the highest average rating at approximately 3.01/5.

# 6. Delivery Time by Product Category

Average delivery times were analysed across product categories.

Electronics had the fastest average delivery time at approximately 6.07 days, while Beauty had the slowest at approximately 6.20 days.

# 7. Delivery Time by Region

Delivery performance was also compared across regions.

The West region had the fastest average delivery time at approximately 6.08 days, while the South region had the slowest at approximately 6.17 days.

The differences were relatively small, suggesting that delivery performance is fairly consistent across regions.

# 8. Revenue by Region and Product Category

Revenue was analysed using both region and product category to identify which combinations performed best.

Electronics was the strongest-performing category across all regions. The South region's Electronics category generated the highest revenue among the region-category combinations, with approximately 487,055.68 in revenue.

# Key Findings

Some of the main findings from the analysis include:

- Electronics is a consistently strong-performing product category across regions.
- Card payments generate the highest amount of revenue compared with COD and Wallet payments.
- The West region has the fastest average delivery time.
- The South region has the slowest average delivery time, although the difference is relatively small.
- Customer ratings are relatively consistent across product categories.
- The South + Electronics combination generated the highest revenue among the region-category combinations.
- Delivery times are generally consistent across both regions and product categories.
